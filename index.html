The driver

Skill, heritage and advocacy.

Loni started racing cars in 2017 after a background in ski racing. She carries the Unser name to a new generation of fans, and she is committed to promoting women in motorsports.

In 2021 she became the first woman to finish in the top 10 in back-to-back races in the Mazda MX-5 Cup.
